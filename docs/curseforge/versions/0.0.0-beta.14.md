<h2>v0.0.0-beta.14 - Full coverage audit</h2>

<p>A full audit re-checked every mod's own <code>es_es.json</code> against its real <code>en_us.json</code>
keys (not just "the file exists") and found 23 mods with a partial, empty, or fake (English copy-pasted
as Spanish) translation shipped in their own jar. All of them are now completed via this resource pack's
overrides.</p>

<h3>Added</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>EvilCraft</strong> (+ <code>evilcraftcompat</code>): 628 + 29 keys.</li>
<li><strong>Xaero's Minimap</strong> (+ <strong>Xaero's Better PVP</strong>): 523 + 18 keys.</li>
<li><strong>Waystones</strong>: 263 keys.</li>
<li><strong>Just Enough Items (JEI)</strong>: 183 keys.</li>
<li><strong>Sophisticated Backpacks</strong>: 143 keys.</li>
<li><strong>Corail Tombstone</strong>: 142 keys.</li>
<li><strong>Sophisticated Storage</strong>: 116 keys.</li>
<li><strong>EnchantmentDescriptions</strong>: 70 keys.</li>
<li><strong>Cyclops Core</strong>: 42 keys (the jar shipped an empty <code>es_es.json</code>).</li>
<li><strong>Mutant Monsters</strong>: 41 keys.</li>
<li><strong>Sodium Extra</strong>: 40 keys.</li>
<li><strong>Dungeons and Taverns</strong> (all sub-mods, shared <code>dnt</code> namespace): 444 keys.</li>
<li><strong>Better Combat</strong>: 21 keys.</li>
<li><strong>Sophisticated Core</strong>: 15 keys.</li>
<li><strong>Curios API</strong> / <strong>Iris</strong>: 12 keys each.</li>
<li><strong>Configured</strong>: 10 keys.</li>
<li><strong>Ecologics</strong>: 7 keys.</li>
<li><strong>Repurposed Structures</strong>: 2 keys. <strong>Nature's Compass</strong> / <strong>Jade</strong>: 1 key each.</li>
<li><strong>New <code>minecraft</code> namespace override</strong>: real Spanish translation of the keys
that Ancient Artifacts 2 injects into vanilla <code>minecraft</code> with English content copy-pasted as
Spanish (death messages, artifact golem/timewarp sounds, "Potion of Expansion/Antigravity"), plus the
Dungeons and Taverns potion effect names.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Fixed</h3>
<ul>
<li>Corrected <code>modid</code> mismatches in <code>docs/TRANSLATIONS_STATUS.md</code>:
<code>ancient_artifacts_mod</code> → <code>ancient_artifacts</code>, and the Dungeons and Taverns
sub-mods now point to their real shared namespace <code>dnt</code> instead of
<code>mr_dungeons_andtaverns*</code>.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Notes</h3>
<blockquote>Ancient Artifacts 2 item/block names still show in English in-game: the mod hardcodes them as
text components in its datapack functions instead of using localizable lang keys, so they cannot be
translated via a standard resource pack. Documented in <code>docs/TRANSLATIONS_STATUS.md</code>.</blockquote>
